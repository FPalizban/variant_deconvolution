# Figure guide

Recommended repository figures:

## Figure 1
Workflow or graphical abstract.

## Figure 2
Model architecture, control-background strategy, and model-version comparison.

## Figure 3
Calibration and evidence-layer ablation.

## Figure 4
Prediction landscape and candidate compartments.

## Figure 5
External pediatric ALL catalog concordance and robustness analyses.

Only upload figures that are aggregate and publication-safe.
